FruitFlyBrain

The fruit-fly brain, from connectome to a fly you can test

Nine projects built on public Drosophila brain data: network analysis of a 3.5-million-synapse connectome, machine-learning models trained on its wiring, and FlyLab, where seven small brains drive NeuroMechFly v2, a fly body built from a micro-CT scan, in MuJoCo physics.

The other projects

  • 01 Connectome graph analysis: hubs and degree structure of the Janelia hemibrain (21,739 neurons).
  • 02 Neurotransmitter atlas: a census of all 139,248 neurons in the FlyWire whole-brain dataset.
  • 03 Pathway tracer: strongest synaptic routes, e.g. Kenyon cells to mushroom-body output neurons.
  • 04 Connectome-constrained dynamics: activity spreading through the real central-complex wiring.
  • 05 3-D neuron viewer: interactive neuron morphologies with NAVis.
  • 06 Cell-type classifier: 88% accuracy over 53 cell-type families from wiring alone.
  • 07 Unsupervised cell typing: neuron embeddings that recover cell types without labels (NMI 0.59).
  • 08 Synapse link prediction: 0.971 ROC-AUC on held-out connections.

Data: Janelia FlyEM hemibrain v1.2 and FlyWire FAFB (CC BY 4.0). Fly body: NeuroMechFly v2 (Wang-Chen et al., Nature Methods 2024), EPFL, Apache-2.0. Code: MIT, Rohit (ROHITCRAFTSYT).
